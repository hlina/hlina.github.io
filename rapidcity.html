Sat 10
Arrive & settle
  • Land RAP 12:26 PM, pick up vehicle(s), lunch.
  • Grocery run for the week — Sam's Club (925 Eglin St, ~7 min) or Walmart/Safeway; stock the Airbnb with breakfast, snacks, toddler food & water.

⚠ Storybook Island often closes for the season ~mid-Oct — verify hours.
2
Sun 11
Mount Rushmore
  • Mount Rushmore National Memorial (~40 min from the Airbnb) — the region's #1 draw, ~2 million visitors/year. Arrive early to beat crowds.
  • Presidential Trail: 0.6-mi loop with ~422 stairs — best views up close, but use a baby carrier (not stroller-friendly). Junior Ranger booklet for the kids; ice cream at Carver's Café (Jefferson's vanilla recipe).
  • Afternoon: naps, or optional Crazy Horse Memorial (~25 min from Rushmore) — the world's largest mountain carving, still in progress.
3
Mon 12
Wildlife — low effort, big payoff
  • Bear Country USA (~20 min from the Airbnb) — ~3-mile drive-through park with black bears, wolves, elk & bison; everyone stays in the car. One of the area's most-loved family stops. 👌 toddlers + pregnancy.
  • Reptile Gardens (~5 min further) — Guinness World Record holder for the world's largest reptile zoo; mostly indoor, easy 1–2 hr visit.
4
Tue 13
Custer State Park — scenic drive
  • Custer State Park — Wildlife Loop Road (~55 min from the Airbnb) — an 18-mile scenic loop through a ~1,300-head bison herd, "begging burros," and prairie dogs, all from the car.
  • Optional flat stroll at Sylvan Lake — the park's "crown jewel"; the Shoreline Trail is an easy ~1.1-mile loop. Turn back early if the kids fade.
5
Wed 14
Badlands National Park
  • Badlands National Park — Loop Road (SD-240) (~60 min from the Airbnb) — ~30 miles of dramatic overlooks right off the road, minimal walking.
  • Toddler-friendly short walks: Door Trail (0.75 mi boardwalk), Window Trail (0.25 mi), Fossil Exhibit Trail (0.25 mi paved). Skip the Notch Trail (log ladder). Junior Ranger badge at the Ben Reifel Visitor Center.
  • Lunch & kitsch at Wall Drug (~10 min from the park) — a roadside icon since 1931, famous for donuts & 5¢ coffee.
6
Thu 15
Rest / indoor flex day
  • Slow morning. Pick one (all ~8–12 min from the Airbnb): Journey Museum (indoor — Native American history & geology), WaTiki Indoor Waterpark (largest indoor waterpark in the Dakotas — toddler zone + lazy river), or Dinosaur Park (free 1936 hilltop landmark, quick photo stop).
  • Good food: Independent Ale House, Que Pasa, or Firehouse Brewing Co.
